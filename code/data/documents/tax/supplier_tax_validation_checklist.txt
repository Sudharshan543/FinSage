# SUPPLIER TAX VALIDATION CHECKLIST

**Document ID:** TAX-CHK-2026-002  
**Document Type:** Supplier Tax Validation Checklist  
**Region:** India  
**Effective Date:** 01 November 2026  
**Access Level:** INTERNAL

## 1. PURPOSE

This checklist supports the review of supplier tax information during invoice verification and accounts payable processing.

## 2. SUPPLIER IDENTIFICATION

Verify the following details:

- Supplier legal name matches the approved vendor master.
- Supplier identification number is recorded.
- GSTIN is available on the invoice.
- Supplier business address is consistent with supporting records.
- Tax registration evidence is available.

## 3. INVOICE TAX VALIDATION

Review the following invoice details:

- Taxable invoice value.
- Applicable CGST and SGST or IGST.
- Tax rates applied.
- Individual tax amounts.
- Total invoice value.
- Mathematical accuracy of tax calculations.

## 4. TAX DOCUMENT VERIFICATION

Check that:

- Supporting tax documents are available.
- GSTIN on the invoice matches the vendor master.
- Tax registration information is supported by available evidence.
- Missing or inconsistent tax details are recorded.
- Unexplained tax differences are referred for further review.

## 5. VALIDATION EXCEPTIONS

The following situations require clarification or escalation:

- Missing GSTIN.
- Invalid or inconsistent supplier tax identification.
- Incorrect tax calculations.
- Missing supporting tax documentation.
- Differences between invoice tax amounts and supporting records.
- Unverified supplier registration changes.

## 6. REVIEW OUTCOME

The reviewer should record one of the following outcomes:

- VERIFIED: Available evidence supports the supplier tax information.
- REQUIRES_CLARIFICATION: Additional information is needed.
- REQUIRES_REVIEW: A discrepancy requires finance or tax team investigation.

## 7. EVIDENCE AND RECORD KEEPING

The reviewer must retain the invoice, supporting tax documents, verification results, exception details, and approval records according to applicable organizational requirements.

## 8. IMPORTANT CONTROL

When evidence is incomplete or contradictory, the reviewer must request clarification rather than assume that the supplier tax information is valid.
