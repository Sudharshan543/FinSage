FINANCE POLICY: EMPLOYEE TRAVEL SAFETY AND EXPENSE CONTROLS

Document ID: FIN-TRV-002
Document Type: Travel Safety Policy
Region: India
Effective Date: 1 November 2026
Access Level: INTERNAL

1. PURPOSE

This policy establishes employee safety and financial control requirements during authorized business travel.

2. TRAVEL APPROVAL

Employees must obtain approval before undertaking business travel.

Travel requests must include the destination, business purpose, travel dates, and estimated expenditure.

3. TRAVEL ARRANGEMENTS

Employees should use approved travel arrangements and reasonable transportation options.

Travel bookings must be supported by confirmations and payment records.

4. ACCOMMODATION

Hotel accommodation must comply with applicable organizational spending limits.

Employees must retain hotel invoices showing accommodation dates, number of nights, room charges, and taxes.

5. TRAVEL EXPENSE DOCUMENTATION

Employees must maintain receipts for transportation, accommodation, meals, and other eligible business expenses.

6. EMERGENCY EXPENSES

Unexpected expenses incurred due to travel emergencies must include an explanation and supporting evidence.

Such expenses may require additional management approval.

7. PERSONAL EXPENSES

Personal purchases and unrelated expenses must not be included in business travel reimbursement claims.

8. EXPENSE REVIEW

Managers must review travel expenses for business purpose, supporting evidence, and compliance with applicable limits.

9. EXCEPTIONS

Unapproved travel, missing receipts, excessive accommodation costs, and unexplained expenses must be recorded and escalated.

10. RECORD RETENTION

Travel authorization, booking confirmations, receipts, expense claims, and approvals must be retained according to applicable organizational requirements.
