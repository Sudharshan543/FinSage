# FINSAGE – VENDOR MASTER DATA MANAGEMENT POLICY

**Policy ID:** FIN-VMD-014  
**Document Type:** Vendor Master Data Policy  
**Region:** India  
**Effective Date:** 01 November 2026  
**Access Level:** INTERNAL

## 1. PURPOSE

This policy establishes procedures for creating, validating, maintaining, and updating vendor master records to support accurate accounts payable processing and prevent unauthorized vendor changes.

## 2. VENDOR REGISTRATION

Before creating a new vendor record, the procurement or vendor management team must obtain:

- Legal business name.
- Registered business address.
- Vendor identification details.
- Applicable GST registration details.
- PAN or other applicable tax identification.
- Authorized contact information.
- Bank account details supported by documentation.

Incomplete vendor applications must be returned for clarification.

## 3. VENDOR IDENTITY VERIFICATION

Vendor identity must be checked against the registration documents and supporting records provided by the vendor.

The vendor's legal name, registration number, address, and tax identification details must be reviewed for consistency.

Unverified or inconsistent information must be referred to the vendor management team before activation.

## 4. BANK ACCOUNT VALIDATION

Bank account details must be supported by appropriate bank documentation.

Changes to existing vendor bank details must undergo independent verification using an established vendor contact channel.

The employee requesting a bank account change must not be the sole person approving that change.

Unverified bank account changes must not be used for payment processing.

## 5. TAX INFORMATION VALIDATION

Applicable supplier tax registration details must be checked against supporting documentation and approved vendor records.

Tax identification mismatches, invalid registration information, and missing tax documents must be recorded for review.

Supplier tax information must be updated when valid supporting evidence is received.

## 6. VENDOR MASTER DATA CHANGES

Changes to vendor names, addresses, tax details, banking information, or authorized contacts must be supported by documented evidence.

All changes must record:

- Previous information.
- Updated information.
- Change request date.
- Requesting employee.
- Verification evidence.
- Approving employee.

## 7. DUPLICATE VENDOR PREVENTION

Before creating a vendor record, the vendor management team must check for existing records using available identifiers, including legal name, tax registration number, and bank account information.

Potential duplicate records must be investigated before a new vendor is activated.

## 8. VENDOR APPROVAL AND ACTIVATION

New vendor records must be reviewed and approved by authorized personnel before activation.

Vendor records with incomplete verification must remain inactive until the required evidence is obtained.

## 9. PERIODIC REVIEW

Vendor master records must be reviewed periodically to identify outdated information, duplicate records, inactive vendors, and incomplete verification records.

Identified discrepancies must be documented and assigned for resolution.

## 10. EXCEPTIONS AND ESCALATION

The following situations require additional review:

- Missing vendor registration documents.
- Invalid or inconsistent tax identification.
- Unverified bank account changes.
- Duplicate vendor records.
- Unauthorized master data changes.
- Incomplete vendor approval records.

Unresolved issues must be escalated to the procurement, finance, or compliance team.

## 11. RECORD RETENTION

Vendor registration documents, verification evidence, approval records, and change history must be retained according to applicable organizational record-retention requirements.

## 12. POLICY INTERPRETATION

When vendor information is incomplete, inconsistent, or unsupported by evidence, the reviewer must request clarification rather than assume that the vendor record is valid.
