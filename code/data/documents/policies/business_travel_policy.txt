
FINSAGE - BUSINESS TRAVEL POLICY

Policy ID: BUSINESS-TRAVEL-002
Region: India
Effective Date: 01 November 2026

1. TRAVEL APPROVAL
All business trips must be approved by the employee's
reporting manager before booking.

2. HOTEL ACCOMMODATION
Hotel expenses must not exceed INR 5,000 per night.
Expenses above this limit require manager approval
and written business justification.

3. MEAL EXPENSES
Employees may claim meal expenses up to INR 1,200
per day during approved business travel.
Original receipts are mandatory.

4. LOCAL TRANSPORTATION
Employees may claim reasonable local transportation
expenses for official business activities.
Valid receipts must be submitted.

5. AIR AND RAIL TRAVEL
Travel bookings must follow the company's approved
travel class and booking guidelines.
Any exception requires prior manager approval.

6. REIMBURSEMENT
Employees must submit expense claims with receipts,
business purpose, travel dates, and employee details.

7. POLICY VIOLATIONS
Claims that exceed approved limits or lack required
documentation must be flagged for review.
