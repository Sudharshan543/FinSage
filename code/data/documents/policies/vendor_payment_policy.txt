FINANCE POLICY: VENDOR PAYMENT AND PAYMENT TERMS

Document ID: FIN-AP-002
Document Type: Vendor Payment Policy
Region: India
Effective Date: 1 November 2026
Access Level: INTERNAL

1. PURPOSE

This policy establishes controls for vendor payment processing, payment scheduling, and supplier account verification.

2. PAYMENT TERM VERIFICATION

Payment terms must be verified using the approved supplier contract, purchase order, or documented agreement.

The accounts payable team must not assume payment terms when supporting evidence is unavailable.

3. INVOICE DUE DATE

The invoice due date must be determined using the payment terms agreed with the supplier.

The invoice date, receipt date, and contractual payment terms must be considered when determining the due date.

4. EARLY PAYMENT DISCOUNTS

Where a supplier offers an early payment discount, the finance team may evaluate whether early payment is beneficial.

The discount must be supported by documented supplier terms.

5. OVERDUE INVOICES

Invoices approaching or exceeding their contractual due dates must be reviewed by the accounts payable team.

Disputed invoices must be clearly identified and escalated for resolution.

6. SUPPLIER BANK ACCOUNT CHANGES

Changes to supplier bank account information must be independently verified using an authorized supplier contact.

Payment must be placed on hold until the change has been verified.

7. PAYMENT APPROVAL

Payments must receive approval according to the applicable delegated authority.

Payment approval must be supported by invoice validation and required purchase order documentation.

8. PAYMENT RECONCILIATION

Completed payments must be reconciled against the corresponding invoice and supplier account.

Differences between payment records and invoice balances must be investigated.

9. EXCEPTION MANAGEMENT

Unverified payment terms, incorrect bank details, duplicate payments, and disputed invoices must be recorded and escalated.

10. RECORD RETENTION

Supplier agreements, payment approvals, payment confirmations, and reconciliation records must be retained according to applicable organizational requirements.
