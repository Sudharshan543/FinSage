FINANCE POLICY: EMPLOYEE REIMBURSEMENT AUDIT AND MONITORING

Document ID: FIN-AUD-001
Document Type: Expense Audit Policy
Region: India
Effective Date: 1 November 2026
Access Level: INTERNAL

1. PURPOSE

This policy establishes procedures for monitoring and auditing employee reimbursement claims.

2. AUDIT SELECTION

Expense claims may be selected for review based on transaction value, expense category, unusual patterns, missing documentation, or policy exceptions.

3. SUPPORTING DOCUMENTATION

Expense claims must be supported by receipts, invoices, booking confirmations, and relevant business justification.

4. DUPLICATE CLAIM DETECTION

Expense reviewers must check for repeated submissions of the same receipt, transaction, date, or amount.

Potential duplicate claims must be investigated before reimbursement.

5. POLICY COMPLIANCE

Reviewers must compare submitted expenses against applicable company policy limits and approval requirements.

6. UNUSUAL EXPENSE PATTERNS

Repeated high-value claims, unusual transaction frequency, inconsistent expense categories, and expenses outside approved travel dates may require additional investigation.

7. AUDIT FINDINGS

Audit findings must include the employee identifier, expense reference, issue description, supporting evidence, and recommended action.

8. EXCEPTION RESOLUTION

Employees may be asked to provide additional documentation or clarification.

Unresolved exceptions must be escalated to the appropriate finance manager.

9. RECORD RETENTION

Audit reports, supporting evidence, investigation notes, and resolution records must be retained according to organizational requirements.

10. CONTINUOUS MONITORING

The finance team should periodically review expense patterns and control effectiveness to identify opportunities for improving reimbursement compliance.
