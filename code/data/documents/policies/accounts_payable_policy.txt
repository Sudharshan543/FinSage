FINANCE POLICY: ACCOUNTS PAYABLE AND INVOICE PROCESSING

Document ID: FIN-AP-001
Document Type: Accounts Payable Policy
Region: India
Effective Date: 1 November 2026
Access Level: INTERNAL

1. PURPOSE

This policy defines the requirements for receiving, validating, approving, and processing supplier invoices. It aims to improve payment accuracy, prevent duplicate payments, and maintain appropriate financial controls.

2. INVOICE SUBMISSION REQUIREMENTS

All supplier invoices must contain:
- Supplier legal name and supplier identification number.
- Unique invoice number.
- Invoice date.
- Description of goods or services supplied.
- Invoice amount and applicable tax details.
- Payment terms.
- Purchase order number, where applicable.
- Supplier bank details registered with the organization.

Invoices with missing mandatory information must be returned to the supplier or placed on hold until the missing information is received.

3. PURCHASE ORDER MATCHING

Invoices associated with purchase orders must be matched against the corresponding purchase order and goods receipt or service confirmation.

The accounts payable team must verify:
- Supplier name matches the purchase order.
- Purchase order number is valid.
- Invoice quantity and description are consistent with the order.
- Invoice amount does not exceed the approved purchase order amount.
- Goods or services have been received or confirmed.

Unexplained differences must be referred to the procurement team for investigation.

4. DUPLICATE INVOICE PREVENTION

Before processing payment, the accounts payable team must check whether the same supplier has previously submitted an invoice with the same invoice number.

A suspected duplicate must be placed on hold until investigated. Payment must not be processed merely because the invoice was resubmitted.

5. PAYMENT APPROVAL

Invoices must be reviewed and approved according to the organization's delegated approval authority.

An invoice must not be approved by the same individual who created or submitted the payment request when segregation of duties is required.

Any invoice exceeding the applicable approval authority must be escalated to the appropriate finance manager.

6. PAYMENT TERMS

Payment due dates must be calculated using the contractual payment terms recorded in the approved supplier agreement or purchase order.

The accounts payable team must not assume standard payment terms when the agreement is unavailable.

Any disagreement concerning payment terms must be referred to procurement or the vendor management team.

7. TAX VALIDATION

Applicable tax registration details and tax amounts must be checked against the supplier invoice and relevant supporting documentation.

Unexplained tax differences must be referred to the tax or finance compliance team before payment approval.

8. EXCEPTIONS AND ESCALATION

The following situations require additional review:
- Missing purchase order information.
- Duplicate invoice numbers.
- Incorrect supplier information.
- Amount differences between invoice and purchase order.
- Missing goods receipt or service confirmation.
- Unverified bank account changes.
- Unclear tax calculations.
- Missing supporting documents.

The reviewer must record the issue, supporting evidence, and resolution status.

9. RECORD RETENTION

Invoice records, purchase orders, approval records, supporting documents, and payment confirmations must be retained according to the organization's applicable records-retention requirements.

10. POLICY INTERPRETATION

If the available invoice evidence is incomplete or contradictory, the accounts payable team must request clarification rather than assume that the invoice is valid.
