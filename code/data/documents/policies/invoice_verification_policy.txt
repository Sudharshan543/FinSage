FINANCE POLICY: INVOICE VERIFICATION AND APPROVAL

Document ID: FIN-AP-003
Document Type: Invoice Verification Policy
Region: India
Effective Date: 1 November 2026
Access Level: INTERNAL

1. PURPOSE

This policy establishes the verification procedures required before supplier invoices can be approved for payment.

2. SUPPLIER VERIFICATION

The accounts payable team must verify the supplier's identity against approved supplier records.

Supplier identification details must be consistent with the purchase order and supplier master data.

3. INVOICE NUMBER VALIDATION

Each invoice must contain a unique invoice number.

Invoices must be checked against previously processed invoices to identify potential duplicates.

4. AMOUNT VALIDATION

The invoice subtotal, applicable taxes, and total amount must be checked for mathematical accuracy.

The invoice amount must be compared with the approved purchase order where applicable.

5. GOODS AND SERVICES CONFIRMATION

The accounts payable team must verify that goods have been received or services have been confirmed.

Invoices without the required confirmation must be held for investigation.

6. TAX DOCUMENTATION

Tax amounts and supplier tax registration information must be reviewed against the supporting invoice documentation.

Unexplained differences must be escalated to the tax team.

7. APPROVAL CONTROLS

Invoices must be approved by authorized personnel before payment processing.

Approval evidence must be recorded and retained.

8. FRAUD PREVENTION

Unusual invoice amounts, unexpected bank account changes, repeated invoice submissions, and inconsistent supplier information must be treated as potential risk indicators.

Such cases must be escalated for additional verification.

9. DISPUTED INVOICES

Disputed invoices must remain on hold until the relevant issue is investigated and resolved.

10. AUDIT EVIDENCE

The organization must retain invoice copies, purchase orders, goods receipt confirmations, approval records, and payment evidence to support financial audits.
