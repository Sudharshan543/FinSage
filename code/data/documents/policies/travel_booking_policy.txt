FINANCE POLICY: BUSINESS TRAVEL BOOKING AND AUTHORIZATION

Document ID: FIN-TRV-001
Document Type: Travel Booking Policy
Region: India
Effective Date: 1 November 2026
Access Level: INTERNAL

1. PURPOSE

This policy defines the requirements for planning, booking, and authorizing business travel.

2. TRAVEL AUTHORIZATION

Employees must obtain the required manager approval before making business travel arrangements.

Travel requests must include the business purpose, destination, expected duration, and estimated cost.

3. TRANSPORTATION

Employees should select reasonable and cost-effective transportation options appropriate for the business trip.

Travel expenses must be supported by valid booking confirmations and payment receipts.

4. HOTEL BOOKING

Hotel accommodation must be appropriate for the business purpose and comply with applicable accommodation limits.

The hotel invoice must identify the accommodation dates, number of nights, room charges, and applicable taxes.

5. TRAVEL CHANGES AND CANCELLATIONS

Changes to approved travel arrangements must be documented.

Cancellation charges must include an explanation of the business reason.

6. LOCAL TRANSPORTATION

Local transportation expenses must be related to authorized business travel.

Employees must provide receipts or other acceptable supporting documentation.

7. TRAVEL EXPENSE CLAIMS

Employees must submit travel expense claims with supporting receipts, booking confirmations, travel dates, and business purpose.

8. POLICY EXCEPTIONS

Travel expenses outside approved arrangements must be recorded and reviewed by the appropriate manager.

9. APPROVAL AND REIMBURSEMENT

Travel expenses must be reviewed and approved before reimbursement.

10. RECORD RETENTION

Travel requests, approvals, booking confirmations, receipts, and expense claims must be retained according to applicable organizational requirements.
