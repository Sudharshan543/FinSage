FINANCE POLICY: PURCHASE ORDER MANAGEMENT

Document ID: FIN-PROC-001
Document Type: Procurement Policy
Region: India
Effective Date: 1 November 2026
Access Level: INTERNAL

1. PURPOSE

This policy establishes controls for creating, approving, modifying, and managing purchase orders.

2. PURCHASE REQUISITIONS

Employees must submit a purchase requisition before committing organizational funds.

The requisition must include the business justification, requested goods or services, estimated cost, department, and required delivery date.

3. PURCHASE ORDER CREATION

An approved purchase requisition must be converted into a purchase order by an authorized procurement employee.

Each purchase order must contain a unique reference number, supplier information, item descriptions, quantities, agreed prices, applicable taxes, and delivery conditions.

4. PURCHASE ORDER APPROVAL

Purchase orders must be approved according to the organization's delegated financial authority.

Employees must not approve purchase requests that exceed their approval limits.

5. SUPPLIER VALIDATION

Suppliers must be checked against approved vendor records before a purchase order is issued.

Supplier identity, registration details, and approved payment information must be verified.

6. PURCHASE ORDER CHANGES

Changes to quantities, prices, delivery dates, or supplier information must be documented and approved.

Significant changes may require a revised purchase order and additional authorization.

7. GOODS RECEIPT

Goods received must be checked against the purchase order.

The receiving employee must record the actual quantity and condition of the goods.

Shortages, damaged goods, and incorrect deliveries must be reported to procurement.

8. SERVICE CONFIRMATION

Services must be confirmed by the responsible department before the related supplier invoice is approved.

The confirmation must identify the service delivered and the relevant purchase order.

9. INVOICE MATCHING

Supplier invoices must be matched against the purchase order and goods receipt or service confirmation.

Differences in quantity, price, or total value must be investigated before payment.

10. PURCHASE ORDER CLOSURE

Purchase orders should be closed when all approved goods or services have been delivered and outstanding invoices have been processed.

11. EXCEPTION MANAGEMENT

Unauthorized purchases, missing purchase orders, unapproved changes, and unmatched invoices must be recorded and escalated to procurement management.
