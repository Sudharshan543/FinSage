DEMO ONLY - FICTIONAL USA BUSINESS TRAVEL POLICY

This document contains invented sample rules for software testing only.
It is not an approved company policy and must not be used to approve,
deny, or reimburse real expenses. All currency limits below are fictional.

Policy ID: DEMO-USA-TRAVEL-001
Document Type: Travel Expense Policy
Region: USA
Effective Date: 1 November 2026
Access Level: INTERNAL

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This test policy demonstrates retrieval of travel, hotel, meal, approval,
receipt, and exception requirements for the USA region. It is not authoritative.

2. TRAVEL APPROVAL AND BOOKING

Business travel must be approved by the employee's reporting manager before
booking. Claims must include the business purpose, destination, travel dates,
and employee details.

Air travel should use economy class and rail travel should use standard coach.
Any exception requires manager approval before booking and a written business
justification.

3. HOTEL ACCOMMODATION

For this demo only, eligible hotel accommodation is reimbursable up to
USD 250 per room per night, before applicable taxes.

Any nightly room rate above USD 250 requires manager approval before booking
and a written business justification.

The hotel invoice must show the hotel name, accommodation dates, number of
nights, nightly room charges, applicable taxes, and proof of payment. A total
hotel amount without the number of nights is insufficient to evaluate the
per-night test limit.

4. MEALS

For this demo only, eligible meals during approved business travel are
reimbursable up to USD 75 per traveler per day.

Meal costs above USD 75 per traveler per day require manager approval and a
written business justification. Meal claims must include an itemized receipt,
expense date, business purpose, and attendee details when applicable.

5. LOCAL TRANSPORTATION

Reasonable local transportation for authorized business activities may be
claimed. Claims must include the expense date, business purpose, route or
service description, amount, and supporting receipt. Personal transportation
and unrelated travel are not reimbursable under this demo policy.

6. RECEIPTS AND CLAIM SUBMISSION

Hotel, meal, air, rail, and local transportation claims must include receipts
or equivalent supporting documentation. Electronic receipts are acceptable
for this test policy. Missing or unreadable documentation must be recorded as
an exception and reviewed before reimbursement.

Expense claims must identify the employee, expense category, date, amount and
currency, business purpose, travel dates, and applicable approval. Hotel claims
must state the number of nights; meal claims must state the number of days and
travelers so per-unit limits can be evaluated.

7. EXCEPTIONS AND APPROVAL

Expenses above the demo limits, missing receipts, or travel booked without
required approval must be referred to an authorized manager for review.
The employee requesting reimbursement must not approve their own claim.

8. POLICY STATUS

The USD 250 hotel limit, USD 75 meal limit, and all other requirements in this
file are fictional test values created for the FinSage academic demonstration.
They are not approved company rules. Replace this file with an approved USA
policy before using FinSage for any real finance or reimbursement decision.
