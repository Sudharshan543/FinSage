FINANCE POLICY: VENDOR ONBOARDING AND MASTER DATA

Document ID: FIN-VEN-001
Document Type: Vendor Management Policy
Region: India
Effective Date: 1 November 2026
Access Level: INTERNAL

1. PURPOSE

This policy defines the controls for registering, validating, and maintaining supplier information.

2. VENDOR REGISTRATION

New suppliers must complete the organization's vendor registration process before receiving purchase orders.

Required information includes:
- Legal business name.
- Registered business address.
- Supplier identification number.
- Applicable tax registration details.
- Authorized contact information.
- Bank account details.

3. DOCUMENT VERIFICATION

Supplier registration documents must be checked for completeness and consistency.

Incomplete or inconsistent information must be returned for clarification.

4. BANK ACCOUNT VERIFICATION

Supplier bank account information must be independently verified before payment details are activated.

Changes to bank details must undergo additional verification using an authorized supplier contact.

5. DUPLICATE VENDOR PREVENTION

The vendor management team must check existing supplier records before creating a new vendor account.

Duplicate supplier registrations must be investigated and resolved.

6. VENDOR APPROVAL

Supplier records must be approved by authorized procurement or vendor management personnel.

Employees must not approve their own vendor registration requests where segregation of duties is required.

7. MASTER DATA MAINTENANCE

Changes to supplier names, addresses, tax details, contacts, and bank information must be documented.

The change history must identify the requester, reviewer, and approval status.

8. VENDOR RISK REVIEW

Unusual supplier requests, inconsistent documentation, and unverified bank account changes must be escalated for additional review.

9. INACTIVE VENDORS

Supplier accounts that are no longer used may be marked inactive according to the organization's vendor management procedures.

10. RECORD RETENTION

Vendor registration documents, verification evidence, approval records, and master data change history must be retained according to applicable organizational requirements.
