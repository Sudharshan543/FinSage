FINANCE POLICY: TAX COMPLIANCE AND INVOICE TAX VALIDATION

Document ID: FIN-TAX-001
Document Type: Tax Compliance Policy
Region: India
Effective Date: 1 November 2026
Access Level: INTERNAL

1. PURPOSE

This policy establishes procedures for reviewing tax information on supplier invoices and maintaining appropriate tax compliance records.

2. TAX IDENTIFICATION

Supplier tax registration details must be verified against approved vendor records and supporting documentation.

3. INVOICE TAX VALIDATION

The accounts payable team must review:
- Supplier tax registration details.
- Taxable invoice value.
- Applicable tax rates.
- Tax amounts.
- Total invoice value.
- Supporting tax documentation.

4. TAX CALCULATION

Tax amounts must be checked for mathematical accuracy.

Differences between the invoice tax calculation and supporting documentation must be investigated.

5. TAX DOCUMENTATION

Supplier invoices must contain the applicable tax details and required supporting information.

Invoices with missing or inconsistent tax information must be placed on hold until clarification is received.

6. TAX REGISTRATION CHANGES

Changes to supplier tax registration information must be documented and independently verified.

7. TAX EXCEPTIONS

Incorrect tax calculations, missing tax details, invalid registration information, and unexplained tax differences must be recorded as compliance exceptions.

8. REVIEW AND ESCALATION

Unresolved tax discrepancies must be referred to the finance or tax compliance team.

9. RECORD RETENTION

Tax invoices, supporting documents, validation records, and exception approvals must be retained according to applicable organizational requirements.

10. POLICY INTERPRETATION

When tax evidence is incomplete or contradictory, the reviewer must request clarification rather than assume compliance.
