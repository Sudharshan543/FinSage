
FIN SAGE - TRAVEL EXPENSE POLICY

Policy ID: TRAVEL-POL-001
Region: India
Effective Date: 01 November 2026

1. HOTEL ACCOMMODATION
Employees may claim hotel accommodation expenses
up to INR 5,000 per night.

Expenses above INR 5,000 require manager approval
and supporting justification.

2. MEALS
Employees may claim meal expenses up to INR 1,200
per day during approved business travel.

Original receipts must be submitted.

3. TRANSPORTATION
Employees may claim reasonable business travel
transportation expenses with valid receipts.

4. DOCUMENTATION
All expense claims must include receipts, business
purpose, expense date, and employee details.

5. EXCEPTIONS
Expenses exceeding policy limits must be flagged
for review and approval by the appropriate manager.
