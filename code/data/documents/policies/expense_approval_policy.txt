FINANCE POLICY: EXPENSE APPROVAL AND AUTHORIZATION

Document ID: FIN-EXP-002
Document Type: Expense Approval Policy
Region: India
Effective Date: 1 November 2026
Access Level: INTERNAL

1. PURPOSE

This policy defines the approval responsibilities and authorization controls for employee expense claims.

2. APPROVAL AUTHORITY

Expense claims must be reviewed and approved by employees with appropriate delegated authority.

Approval limits must be followed according to the organization's financial authorization matrix.

3. MANAGER REVIEW

Managers must review the business purpose, expense category, amount, date, and supporting documentation before approving a claim.

4. SEGREGATION OF DUTIES

Employees must not approve their own expense claims.

Where required, separate individuals must perform expense submission, review, and payment authorization.

5. HIGH-VALUE EXPENSES

Expenses exceeding the applicable approval threshold must be escalated to the appropriate finance manager.

6. POLICY EXCEPTIONS

Expenses exceeding policy limits must include a documented explanation and additional approval.

7. MISSING DOCUMENTATION

Claims without receipts or other required supporting documents must be returned for clarification or recorded as exceptions.

8. APPROVAL RECORDS

Approval records must include the reviewer identity, approval date, decision, and comments where applicable.

9. REJECTED CLAIMS

Rejected claims must include a reason so the employee can understand the required correction.

10. AUDIT AND RETENTION

Expense submissions, supporting evidence, approvals, rejections, and exception records must be retained according to applicable organizational requirements.
