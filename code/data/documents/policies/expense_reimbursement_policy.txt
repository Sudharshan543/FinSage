FINANCE POLICY: EMPLOYEE EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT

Document ID: FIN-EXP-001
Document Type: Expense Reimbursement Policy
Region: India
Effective Date: 1 November 2026
Access Level: INTERNAL

1. PURPOSE

This policy defines the requirements for submitting, reviewing, approving, and reimbursing employee business expenses.

2. ELIGIBLE EXPENSES

Employees may claim reasonable business expenses incurred while performing authorized work-related activities.

Eligible categories may include:
- Business travel.
- Hotel accommodation.
- Meals during approved business travel.
- Local transportation.
- Approved business-related purchases.

3. EXPENSE SUBMISSION

Employees must submit expense claims with:
- Employee identification.
- Expense category.
- Expense date.
- Amount and currency.
- Business purpose.
- Supporting receipts or invoices.
- Required approval details.

4. RECEIPT REQUIREMENTS

Original receipts or acceptable supporting documentation must be provided for expense claims.

Claims without supporting documentation must be reviewed and may require additional clarification.

5. HOTEL EXPENSES

Hotel expenses must comply with the applicable accommodation limits established by the organization.

The reviewer must consider the number of nights, room rate per night, location, and supporting hotel invoice.

A total hotel amount alone is insufficient to establish whether the nightly limit was exceeded.

6. MEAL EXPENSES

Meal expenses must comply with the applicable daily limits.

The reviewer must consider the expense date, number of days, meal category, and supporting receipts.

A combined meal amount without a corresponding duration cannot independently establish daily compliance.

7. BUSINESS TRAVEL APPROVAL

Business travel must receive the required authorization before expenses are incurred.

Employees must follow the organization's approved travel arrangements and applicable spending limits.

8. PROHIBITED EXPENSES

Personal purchases and expenses unrelated to authorized business activities are not eligible for reimbursement.

9. EXCEPTION HANDLING

Expenses exceeding applicable limits or missing required supporting documents must be recorded as exceptions.

Exceptions must include a reason, supporting evidence, and appropriate approval status.

10. REIMBURSEMENT APPROVAL

Expense claims must be reviewed by authorized personnel before reimbursement.

Employees must not approve their own expense claims where segregation of duties is required.

11. RECORD RETENTION

Expense claims, receipts, approvals, and reimbursement records must be retained according to applicable organizational requirements.
