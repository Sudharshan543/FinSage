DEMO ONLY - FICTIONAL USA VENDOR ONBOARDING POLICY

This document contains invented sample requirements for software testing only.
It is not an approved company policy and must not be used to approve or reject
real suppliers, activate payment details, or make tax or legal decisions.

Policy ID: DEMO-USA-VENDOR-001
Document Type: Vendor Onboarding Policy
Region: USA
Effective Date: 1 November 2026
Access Level: INTERNAL

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This test policy demonstrates retrieval of vendor registration, document
verification, bank verification, duplicate prevention, approval, and record
maintenance requirements for the USA region. It is not authoritative.

2. VENDOR REGISTRATION

For this demo only, a new supplier must complete the sample vendor registration
before receiving a purchase order. The sample registration records:

- Legal business name and registered business address.
- Supplier identification and applicable tax identification details.
- Applicable tax forms or supporting tax documents, when required by the
  organization's approved process.
- Authorized business contact information.
- Payment and bank account details supported by documentation.

These are illustrative test fields, not a statement of USA legal requirements.

3. DOCUMENT AND IDENTITY VERIFICATION

Registration documents must be checked for completeness and consistency with
the submitted supplier identity and tax information. Incomplete, inconsistent,
or unsupported information must be returned for clarification and must not be
treated as verified.

4. BANK ACCOUNT VERIFICATION

Bank account information must be independently verified before payment details
are activated. Changes to bank details require additional verification through
an established supplier contact channel. Unverified changes must not be used
for payment processing.

5. DUPLICATE VENDOR PREVENTION

Before creating a supplier record, the vendor management team must check
available identifiers, including legal name, tax identifier, and bank account
details, against existing records. Potential duplicates must be investigated
before a new record is activated.

6. REVIEW AND APPROVAL

Supplier records must be reviewed and approved by authorized procurement or
vendor management personnel before activation. Employees must not approve
their own vendor requests where segregation of duties is required.

7. MASTER DATA CHANGES

Changes to supplier name, address, tax details, contacts, or bank information
must be supported by documentation and record the requester, reviewer, and
approval status. Sensitive bank changes require independent verification.

8. EXCEPTIONS AND RECORDS

Missing documents, inconsistent supplier information, unverified bank details,
and potential duplicates must be referred for review. Registration documents,
verification evidence, approvals, and change history must be retained according
to the organization's approved record-retention requirements.

9. POLICY STATUS

Every requirement in this file is fictional test content created for the
FinSage academic demonstration. It is not approved corporate policy or legal,
tax, or compliance guidance. Replace it with an approved USA vendor onboarding
policy before using FinSage for real supplier decisions.
